# Hardware

## Parts

| Part | Notes |
|---|---|
| Raspberry Pi 4 | Raspberry Pi OS Lite, 64-bit (Debian 13 "trixie"), kernel 6.18 (`rpt-rpi-v8`). Powered over USB-C. |
| RaspiAudio ULTRA++ HAT | WM8960 codec over I2S. The board is marked "ULTRA V3"; its EEPROM reads "ULTRA V2". Onboard microphones, mic jack and speaker amplifier are not used. |
| 4.0" SPI display module | 320 x 480, ST7796S controller, XPT2046 resistive touch. The project calls it KMRTM40045-SPI; the enclosure takes its dimensions from the lcdwiki MSP4021 drawing and checks them with calipers. |
| Two 1/4" panel jacks | IN and OUT. 3/8-32 bushing, plastic body. Each is wired to a 3.5 mm plug that sits in the HAT's LINE IN or headphone socket. |
| Printed enclosure | Lid, base and optional spacers: see [`enclosure/`](../enclosure/). |

Signal path: panel IN, HAT LINE IN (left channel), codec, engine, codec, HAT headphone out, panel
OUT. See [audio-path.md](audio-path.md).

## Display wiring

The panel sits on SPI0 (the panel on CE0, the touch controller on CE1) and is wired with jumpers to
the HAT's pass-through header.

| Display pin | Pi pin | GPIO |
|---|---|---|
| VCC | 1 (3.3 V), see below | - |
| GND | 6 | - |
| CS / RESET / D/C | 24 / 22 / 18 | 8 (CE0) / 25 / 24 |
| SDI / SCK | 19 / 23 | 10 / 11 |
| LED (backlight) | 17 (3.3 V), always on | - |
| SDO (MISO) | not connected: it does not tri-state and would corrupt touch reads | - |
| T_CLK / T_DIN / T_OUT | 23 / 19 / 21 | 11 / 10 / 9 |
| T_CS / T_IRQ | 26 / 11 | 7 (CE1) / 17 |

GPIOs in use: the display takes 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 17, 24 and 25; the HAT takes 2 and 3 (I2C) and
18 to 21 (I2S); UART 14 and 15 is enabled; 5 and 6 are the jack sense. 23 reads high with a
pull-down, so the HAT probably drives it. Free: 4, 12, 13, 16, 22, 26, 27. Never use GPIO 2 or 3:
they are the HAT's I2C, and grounding them kills the codec.

**3.3 V only.** The display board's 5 V to 3.3 V regulator (U1, marked "662K") died after the board
was powered once with VCC and GND reversed. It was repaired by bridging the solder jumper J1 next
to U1 and running VCC from the Pi's 3.3 V pin. J1 now passes the supply straight to the 3.3 V
parts, so 5 V on VCC would destroy the board and feed back into the Pi's GPIO.

## Display software

- `config/display.txt` is appended to `/boot/firmware/config.txt`: `spi=on`, the `mipi-dbi-spi`
  overlay (ST7796S, 320 x 480, reset on GPIO25, D/C on GPIO24, 48 MHz SPI clock) and the `ads7846`
  overlay for the touch controller (CS 1, IRQ on GPIO17).
- `/lib/firmware/st7796s.bin` holds the panel's init sequence, generated by
  `display/make_st7796s_bin.py --rotate=180`. Rotation 180 puts the UI upright in the enclosure.
  The result is a plain framebuffer (`/dev/fb0`, RGB565) and a DRM device the UI draws to.
- Touch is the kernel's `ads7846` driver, read from evdev and mapped to pixels with an affine
  calibration (`display/touchtest.py`, five crosshairs; stored in
  `/var/lib/bassbox/touch_cal.json`, seeded from `config/touch_cal.json`). Accuracy was about 1 px
  at the calibration points and about 9 px on average in the tap game (19 of 20 hits). Touches
  near the left edge read 7 to 15 px too far left, so no interactive target starts before x = 8.
- The XPT2046 bounces on release (extra down/up pairs) and its position drifts over the last few
  samples before lift-off. `display/screen.py` and the UI's touch thread filter both:
  press length, a bounce window, and the median of the press minus its last samples.

## Jack sense

GPIO5 (pin 29) senses the IN jack and reads 1 when a plug is in. GPIO6 (pin 31) senses the OUT
jack; with a real amp cable it reads 1 when plugged (measured on 2026-10-18; an earlier reading of
0 came from the sleeve of a loopback cable). Polarity is set in `config/jacks.conf`. Internal
pull-ups are on and the engine debounces for 100 ms. With the IN jack empty the engine fades the
output to silence, and the UI shows the plug popping out of its socket.
